Room Regulations

  • Phones and smart watches must be OFF and in bags.
  • Place Dundee ID card on the desk.
  • Enter in silence and stay silent until instructed.

Online Procedures

  • Exams run on MyDundee; bring a charged laptop.
  • Only one browser tab allowed (MyDundee).
  • No translation or auto-translation software allowed.

Leaving & Timing

  • No leaving for exams shorter than one hour.
  • For longer exams, leaving is usually allowed after 30 minutes and not in the final 15 minutes.
  • Emergency exits normally mean no re-entry.

Allowed Equipment

  • Black/blue pens and a ruler.
  • Scientific calculator (non-graphing).
  • For listening exams, bring working headphones.
High-risk misconduct: using translation tools, having extra tabs open, or having a phone/watch on your person.

When to Reference

  • Direct quotes from websites/books/journals.
  • Paraphrasing other people's ideas.
  • Reusing your own previously published work.

Required Components

  • In-text citation: Author and Year.
  • Reference List: Full source details at the end.
  • Large-scale AI-generated writing may also trigger misconduct concerns.

Practical Submission Safety

  • Track your source list while reading, not at the end.
  • Reference paraphrases, not only direct quotes.
  • Check Turnitin workflow before group submissions.

Application Route

Email SSEN-DIICSU@dundee.ac.uk with subject: Mitigating Circumstances - Private and Confidential.

Required Information

  • Student details, affected modules, and assessment titles.
  • Clear impact explanation with dated evidence.
  • Submission status and relevant dates.

Timing and Consent

  • Deadlines are usually announced each semester.
  • Committee/adviser discussion may require consent.
  • Submit evidence early for stronger review.

When to Use This Route

  • Use for serious impact, not routine deadline pressure.
  • If equipment failure blocks online exams, this route may be required.
