Exam Regulations

Strict protocols for room behaviour and online platforms.

General room rules

  • Phones and Smart Watches must be OFF and stored in bags.
  • Enter in silence and place ID card on the desk.
  • Late entry allowed only if no student has left; no extra time.

Leaving and timing rules

  • You cannot leave an exam that lasts less than one hour.
  • For longer exams, leaving is usually only allowed after 30 minutes and not in the last 15 minutes.
  • Emergency exits normally mean re-entry is not allowed.

Online Setup

  • Run on MyDundee; bring a charged laptop.
  • Only one tab allowed: MyDundee.
  • No translation or auto-translation software.

Allowed equipment

  • Three black or blue pens and a ruler.
  • A scientific calculator (not graphing).
  • For listening exams, bring working headphones and use provided note paper only.
High-risk mistakes: keeping extra tabs open, using translation tools, or carrying a watch/phone on your person. These are academic misconduct.

Referencing & Citation

Maintaining integrity using the Harvard Style.

Requirements

  • Reference all copied, paraphrased, or reused work.
  • Includes In-text citation (Author, Year) and full Reference List.

What Harvard style includes

  • In-text citation: author plus year where the source is used.
  • Reference list: full source details at the end, usually sorted alphabetically.
  • Large-scale AI-generated writing can also be treated as misconduct in module rules.

Tools & Guides

MendeleyOrganize and insert citations. Harvard GuideReference examples and formats. Cite Them RightOfficial Harvard guide.

Practical advice for safe submissions

  • Keep a source list while reading instead of rebuilding it at the end.
  • Reference paraphrases, not just direct quotations.
  • Check Turnitin submission rules before group uploads.

Mitigating Circumstances

Formal route for serious illness or emergencies.

How to Submit

Email SSEN-DIICSU@dundee.ac.uk with subject: Mitigating Circumstances - Private and Confidential.

Required Info

  • Student details, affected modules, and assessment titles.
  • Clear explanation of impact with dated evidence attached.

Timing and consent

  • Deadlines are usually set near the end of each semester and communicated in advance.
  • You may need consent for discussion with your Student Adviser and committee review.
  • Submit evidence early; late evidence often weakens the application.

When to use this route

  • Use it for genuine serious impact, not normal deadline pressure.
  • If equipment failure blocks an online exam, this route may be required before a retake.
